Frequently Asked Questions about iAbhyas JEE Prep

Everything you need to know about AI-Powered Diagnostic Tests and adaptive learning

How does AI analyze my JEE performance on iAbhyas?
Our AI engine tracks more than just correctness. It monitors the time you spend on each question, your scrolling patterns, the difficulty transition, and the sequence of steps. This allows the AI to determine if a wrong answer was caused by a conceptual gap, a calculation slip, or time-pressure panic.
Are the mock tests based on the latest NTA pattern?
Yes, absolutely. All iAbhyas JEE Main and JEE Advanced Mock Tests are fully aligned with the latest National Testing Agency (NTA) guidelines, including both Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) and Numerical Value Questions (with optional selectors where applicable).
Can I practice only weak chapters?
Yes! That is the core advantage of our Personalized JEE Practice Hub. After your diagnostic assessment, the system generates targeted, chapter-wise questions focusing solely on concepts that need repair, ensuring you don't waste time on topics you've already mastered.
How often should I attempt full-length mock tests?
We recommend taking a diagnostic mock test once every two weeks in the initial months to highlight concept loopholes. As the JEE exam approaches (last 60 days), you should increase this to two full-length simulated mock tests per week.
Is iAbhyas suitable for both JEE Main and JEE Advanced?
Yes. iAbhyas provides two distinct difficulty algorithms. The JEE Main module focuses on accuracy and speedy calculation. The JEE Advanced module focuses on multi-concept synthesis problems, testing your structural reasoning and deep physics/math fundamentals.
How is my predicted JEE percentile calculated?
Our predictive model compares your accuracy, speed vector, and concept mastery index against historical performance data of over 100,000 past aspirants who have taken actual NTA exams. This provides a highly accurate predicted percentile, usually within a +/- 1.5% margin of error.
How many mock tests are available on iAbhyas?
We offer over 500+ mock tests in total, which include 50+ Full-Length National Mock Tests, 150+ Part-Syllabus Tests, and over 300+ Chapter-wise Adaptive Practice Papers.
Can I use iAbhyas on my mobile phone?
Yes! The iAbhyas interface is fully optimized and mobile-responsive, allowing you to read revision notes, practice daily questions (DPPs), and check your performance dashboard on any smartphone or tablet.
How are the diagnostic reports generated and shared?
Immediately upon completing any diagnostic test, your interactive dashboard updates. A detailed PDF version, which breaks down concepts and gives step-by-step guidance, is also emailed to you and your registered parents.
How does the adaptive practice feature work?
When you begin a session, our system serves you medium-difficulty questions. If you answer correctly, the next question becomes slightly more challenging (testing application limits). If you make a mistake, the difficulty eases and serves fundamental problems to reinforce basics.
What is the marking scheme used in iAbhyas tests?
For JEE Main, we employ the official +4 for correct and -1 for incorrect answers. For JEE Advanced, we support complex marking schemes, including partial marks, negative marks, and zero marks depending on the specific question block configuration.
Does iAbhyas offer doubt resolution support?
Yes! Every question in our JEE Mock Test database features detailed step-by-step written solutions. For premium subscribers, we offer 24x7 instant AI-assisted tutoring that explains the precise physics, chemistry, or math concept step-by-step.
Is the initial AI Diagnostic Test completely free?
Yes, the initial diagnostic assessment is 100% free with no credit card required. It is designed to help you identify your conceptual weaknesses and experience the iAbhyas learning engine firsthand.
Can I bookmark difficult questions for future revision?
Yes, you can save any question to your personalized revision folder with a single click. You can also add custom study notes to bookmarked questions for revision before exam days.
How does iAbhyas compare to standard offline test series?
Standard offline series provide static rankings and simple score cards weeks after the test. iAbhyas provides instant feedback, pinpointing the precise sub-topic gaps, and gives you a dynamic daily schedule to patch those gaps instantly.
What are the system requirements for online CBT mock tests?
Since our platform is fully cloud-based, it runs seamlessly in any modern web browser (such as Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox) on laptops, desktops, or tablets. No heavy software installations are needed.
Are numerical value questions included in the chapter practice?
Yes, numerical value questions, where students must compute and input the exact numeric answer, are a key feature of our question banks to ensure perfect compatibility with NTA guidelines.
